About

Always up for something new.

I like a challenge, and I tend to work better under pressure. A tight deadline focuses me in a way that open-ended time never does.

I'm naturally curious, so I'm usually picking up something new: a hobby, a topic I fell down a rabbit hole on, a skill I'm not good at yet. I like the early stage, when everything is unfamiliar and every bit of progress is noticeable.

Reading is my favorite way to slow down. I'm drawn to anything that makes me think, whether that's books, puzzles, or questions without clean answers. I'd rather finish something with more questions than I started with than a neat conclusion.

I like meeting new people. Everyone knows something I don't, and conversations are where I find half the things I end up getting into. I'm happy with where my life is right now, and I'm lucky to have people who love me. It's a big part of why trying new things feels easy.
